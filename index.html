Who is the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?

Stages of the embryo

What people believed. For most of human history, no one knew how a human is formed. Aristotle (4th century BC) taught that the embryo forms from the mother's menstrual blood, shaped by the father's semen. Galen (2nd century AD), whose work guided medicine for 1,400 years, held similar views. Even in the 17th and 18th centuries, with microscopes available, many scientists believed that a tiny, fully formed human (a “homunculus”) sat inside the sperm and only grew larger. Karl Ernst von Baer discovered the mammalian egg in 1827. It was only in the 20th century that the stages of the human embryo were described and classified in detail.

What the Quran says. More than 1,000 years before any of that was described, God says:

“We created man from an extract of clay. Then We placed him as a drop (nutfa) in a firm resting place. Then We made the drop into a clinging thing ('alaqa), then We made the clinging thing into a chewed-like lump (mudgha), then We made the lump into bones, then clothed the bones with flesh, then We produced it as another creation. So blessed is God, the best of creators.”Quran 23:12-14

  • A drop in a firm resting place. The fertilised egg settles and implants in the wall of the uterus.
  • 'Alaqa. The word means something that clings, and also a leech or a blood clot. In the first weeks, the embryo attaches to the uterine wall, receives blood and looks like a leech in shape.
  • Mudgha. A chewed-like lump. At about 4 weeks the embryo is small, with the rows of somites on its back looking like teeth marks in a chewed substance.
  • Bones clothed with flesh. The skeleton forms as cartilage, and the muscles then form around it.
  • “Another creation.” The form becomes recognisably human, with the face, limbs and organs.

The Prophet ﷺ described the same sequence:

“The creation of each of you is gathered in his mother's womb for forty days as a drop, then a clinging thing for a like period, then a chewed-like lump for a like period…”Sahih al-Bukhari 3208, Sahih Muslim 2643

More details in the Quran.

“Indeed, We created man from a mixed drop, to test him, and We made him hearing and seeing.”Quran 76:2

“And that He creates the two mates, the male and the female, from a drop when it is emitted.”Quran 53:45-46

“He creates you in the wombs of your mothers, creation after creation, in three darknesses.”Quran 39:6

  • A mixed drop (76:2) describes the joining of the male and female gametes. It also lists hearing before sight (see also 32:9). The inner ear starts to function before the eyes can see.
  • Sex is determined by the sperm (53:45-46). It was only in the early 20th century that scientists found that the father's sperm, carrying X or Y chromosomes, determines the sex of the child.
  • Three darknesses (39:6). Scientists describe three layers around the fetus: the abdominal wall, the wall of the uterus and the membranes around the embryo.

The expert who confirmed it. Dr. Keith L. Moore (1925-2019) was a professor of anatomy at the University of Toronto and the author of The Developing Human, one of the most widely used embryology textbooks in the world. In 1980 he was invited to Saudi Arabia, where he worked with the Embryology Committee of King Abdulaziz University and answered questions about the embryology described in the Quran and hadith. He said that it had been a pleasure to help clarify the Quran's statements about human development, and a special edition of his textbook was published with the Quranic verses and hadith alongside.
